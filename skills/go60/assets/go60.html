Layout notes from the Layout Editor

Legends show what each key types with the Finnish keyboard layout. Positions use MoErgo's names: C1 is the inner column (index finger) and C6 the outer pinky column, R1 is the number row, and the thumb keys T1–T3 continue the bottom arc toward the middle (T1 next to C2R5). The drawing is schematic; in a wide window the thumb keys are shown where they really are.

On the keyboard

After saving, run go60-build to put the presets on the keyboard. They come after MoErgo's effects; hold Magic (LH C6R4) and tap LH C1R3 to step to the next one:

    Brightness keys (Magic + LH C2R2 / C2R3) dim presets too; the colour and saturation keys only affect MoErgo's effects.
